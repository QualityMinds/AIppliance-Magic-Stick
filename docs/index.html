Good to know

A few things
worth knowing.

Your platform. Informed decisions.

Do I need a GPU?+

Not for the base platform. You can run small models on a supported CPU or connect external model services. Local GPU model serving requires suitable hardware and the relevant configuration. See the compatibility reference.

Where do the models come from?+

Magic Stick can connect external model endpoints through LiteLLM and run local models through KubeAI with Ollama or vLLM, or directly with FreeToken. The model source depends on your setup.

Does all data stay on my infrastructure?+

Not automatically. If you connect external model services, requests can leave your infrastructure. Data flows depend on the applications, model sources and configuration you choose. Installation and downloads also use external sources.

Who is responsible for running the platform?+

You or your organisation remain responsible for infrastructure and operations. Your IT team or an external service provider can support you.

How does sign-in work?+

Magic Stick uses Keycloak and Envoy Gateway for central sign-in to the dashboard and appropriately configured application routes. Application-specific permissions remain within the applications.

What technical knowledge do I need?+

Installation and ongoing operation need technical ownership. The exact requirements depend on your deployment route. If you are planning a team deployment, involve your IT team or service provider early.

What runs under the hood?+

Kubernetes with Flux keeps the platform configured. The Magic Stick Operator prepares model runtimes from your dashboard settings, and LiteLLM gives applications one model API. Read the architecture overview for the details.

Where can I get help?+

Start with the troubleshooting guides and the support policy. Project support is best-effort, without a guaranteed response time. Keep credentials and private deployment information out of public issues.
